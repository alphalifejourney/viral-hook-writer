# Example: Write hooks for a dermatology clinic

**You type:**
> Write hooks for my dermatology clinic. The video is about why adults still get acne. My viewers are working women aged 25-40.

**The skill replies:**

**Hooks for: Dermatology clinic – Why adults still get acne**

1. [Myth] Acne stops after your teens? Not true. – 10/10
2. [Contrast] Pimples at 16 or at 32? What changed? – 10/10
3. [Question] Why do you still get pimples at 30? – 9/10
4. [Mistake] Stop [habit from your video] if your pimples keep coming back. – 9/10
5. [Behind the scenes] This is what's really behind your adult acne. – 9/10
6. [Challenge] Try guessing why your skin still breaks out. – 9/10
7. [Warning] Don't cover another breakout until you watch this. – 9/10
8. [Surprising fact] You can get acne for the first time at 30. – 9/10
9. [Question] Why do your pimples keep coming back? – 8/10
10. [Pointing at viewer] If you're 30 and still breaking out, this is for you. – 7/10

**Top 3**
🥇 Acne stops after your teens? Not true. – It breaks a belief many grown women hold, so they want to know why they're still breaking out.
🥈 Pimples at 16 or at 32? What changed? – Comparing teen pimples with pimples at 32 makes viewers want the reason.
🥉 Why do you still get pimples at 30? – It asks the exact question your viewers have, and the video answers it.

**First second on screen (🥇):** A woman in work clothes looks closely at a pimple in a mirror, then a big "NOT TRUE" stamp pops onto the screen.

**Fill in the brackets:** In hook 4, swap [habit from your video] for an everyday habit your video actually talks about, like a skincare or makeup habit you cover.

**Tip:** put the hook as big text on screen; many people watch with the sound off.

---

**Why this is safe for a skin clinic:** no hook promises a cure or a result, and none invents facts about the clinic. The one detail the skill doesn't know is left in [brackets] for you to fill in.
