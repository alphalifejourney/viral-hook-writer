# Example: Generate Hooks

**You type:**
> Write hooks for my bakery. The video is about our new mango cake. My customers are young families.

**The skill replies (shortened):**

```
HOOKS FOR: Bakery – New mango cake

1. [Mistake] Stop buying birthday cakes that kids don't finish. — 8/10
2. [Question] Why do kids always leave the cake and eat the icing? — 7/10
3. [Contrast] Chocolate cake vs our new mango cake. Kids voted. — 9/10
4. [Behind the scenes] This is how 4kg of fresh mango becomes one cake. — 8/10
...

TOP 3
🥇 Chocolate cake vs our new mango cake. Kids voted. — Simple "vs" plus a fun result parents want to see.
🥈 Stop buying birthday cakes that kids don't finish. — Hits a real parent problem.
🥉 This is how 4kg of fresh mango becomes one cake. — Shows quality without bragging.

FIRST SECOND ON SCREEN (for 🥇): Two cake slices side by side, a kid's hand reaching for one.
```
