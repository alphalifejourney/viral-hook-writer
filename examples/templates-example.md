# Example: Hook templates for a dermatology clinic

**You type:**
> Show me hook templates for my dermatology clinic

**The skill replies:**

Swap the words in [brackets] for your own details. 🔒 means use it only with a real fact, number or quote you have. "Result first" is left out because before/after promises are risky for skin and health businesses.

| # | Formula | Template | Example (dermatology clinic) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Question | Why does your [thing] always [annoying problem]? | Why do your pimples keep coming back? |
| 2 | Mistake | Stop [common habit] if you want [what they want]. | Stop popping pimples if you want fewer marks. |
| 3 | Myth | [Common belief]? Not true. | Acne stops after your teens? Not true. |
| 4 | Contrast | [Option A] or [option B]? [Short question]. | Cream or gel moisturizer? Which suits your skin? |
| 5 | Warning | Don't [action] until you watch this. | Don't pop that pimple until you watch this. |
| 6 | Pointing at viewer | If you [situation], this is for you. | If you're always covering breakouts, this is for you. |
| 7 | Behind the scenes | This is what [process] really looks like. | This is what a [treatment name] really looks like. |
| 8 | Challenge | Try [doing something] without [reaction]. | Try counting how many times you touch your face today. |
| 9 | Surprising fact | [True, surprising fact]. | You can get acne for the first time at 30. |
| 10 | How-to | How to [get result] in [real time/steps]. | How to put on sunscreen in [number] easy steps. |
| 11 | 🔒 Story | A customer told us [real thing they said]. | A patient told us [their real words]. |
| 12 | 🔒 Number | [Real number] [things/people/times] [fact]. | [Real number] people asked us about [skin concern] this month. |

Never promise a cure or a guaranteed result. Use words like "can help", "common cause" or "ask us about".

**Tips**
- Say the hook out loud. If it sounds weird to say, rewrite it.
- Show something moving or surprising in the first second.
- Put the hook as big text on screen. Many people watch with the sound off.
