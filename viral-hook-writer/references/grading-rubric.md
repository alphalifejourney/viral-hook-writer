# Hook Grading Rubric

Score every hook with all 5 checks (0, 1 or 2 points each), then add them up for a total out of 10. Always add the points; never guess a total.

## How to count words

- Split on spaces. "I'm", "90%", "$3", "9am" and "vs" each count as one word.
- A [bracket placeholder] counts as one word, however many words are inside.
- For languages without spaces between words (like Khmer or Thai), use speaking time at a normal pace instead: up to 3 seconds = "1–8 words", 3–4.5 seconds = "9–12 words", over 4.5 seconds = "13 or more words". Estimate; don't ask the user to time it. Rough guide for Khmer: about 15 syllables ≈ 3 seconds.

## What counts as filler

Filler is an opening that tells the viewer nothing yet:
- greetings: "Hi everyone", "Hey guys", "Welcome back"
- warm-ups: "So", "Today", "In this video", "Did you know", "Most people don't know"
- self-introductions: the business's own name, "We are…", "Our [business/team] is…", "Our treatment…" (an "Our…" line with a real surprise, like "Our oven caught fire", is not filler)

## The 5 checks

### 1. Curiosity – Does it make people want to know what happens next?
| Points | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Opens a clear question the video answers | "Why does your iced coffee taste watery?" |
| 1 | Somewhat interesting, but the answer is easy to guess | "Our new iced coffee is here." |
| 0 | Gives everything away, or nothing to wonder about | "We sell coffee. Come visit." |

### 2. Clarity – Can a 12-year-old understand it the first time?
| Points | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Plain, everyday words | "Stop brushing like this." |
| 1 | Needs a second read, or has one unusual word | "Stop compromising your enamel like this." |
| 0 | Jargon, or confusing | "Optimise your oral microbiome synergy." |

### 3. Speed – Does it get to the point fast?
| Points | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 2 | 1–8 words, no filler |
| 1 | 9–12 words, no filler |
| 0 | 13 or more words, OR it starts with filler |

### 4. Relevance – Does it fit THIS video and THIS viewer?
| Points | Meaning | Example (cake video for parents) |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Names a problem, wish or situation the viewer has AND connects to the video topic. It doesn't need to name the viewer group. | "Why do kids leave the cake and eat the icing?" |
| 1 | Fits the viewer OR the topic, not both | "Parents, this one's for you." |
| 0 | Could be about anything | "You won't believe this." |

If you don't know who the viewer is, Relevance can be **at most 1**. Say so.

### 5. Contrast – Does it surprise, challenge, or compare?
| Points | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | A clear surprise, a belief proven wrong, or an "A vs B" | "Bleeding gums aren't normal." |
| 1 | A small twist or a gentle challenge | "Stop brushing like this." |
| 0 | Totally expected | "Brush your teeth twice a day." |

## Honesty check (every hook, including the user's own)

There are two levels. Show each flag and explain it in one plain sentence (one sentence per problem).

When a cap applies, still add up the 5 checks, then show both numbers so the table and total agree:
**Score: 3/10 (points 8, capped) – ⛔ Don't post this**

**⚠️ Unproven claim → total capped at 5/10**
Something specific that could be false and that the user hasn't confirmed:
- statistics, amounts or results ("90% of gyms…", "doubled our sales")
- customer quotes or stories
- "best", "#1", "only"
- attacks on competitors or the user's own industry

The cap is removed if the user confirms the claim is true and can be proven.
Ordinary numbers like ages, times or steps ("at 30", "3 steps") are not claims.

**⛔ Risky promise → total capped at 3/10, always**
- cures, guarantees, or "results in X days"
- health, beauty, money or safety promises ("clears acne", "make $1,000 a week")
- before/after claims for health or beauty

This cap is never removed, even if the user says it's true. Platforms and health rules can punish these claims. Suggest a safe version ("can help", "common cause", "ask us about").

## Score labels

| Total | Label | What to tell the user |
|---|---|---|
| 9–10 | ✅ Ready to post | Use it. |
| 7–8 | 👍 Good | One small change could make it great. |
| 5–6 | 🔧 Needs work | Needs a clearer problem or a stronger twist. |
| 0–4 | ❌ Rewrite | Start again with a template. |

Capped hooks use these labels instead, because the problem is the claim, not the writing:

| Flag | Label | What to tell the user |
|---|---|---|
| ⚠️ | ⚠️ Fix the claim first | Prove it or remove it, then re-grade. |
| ⛔ | ⛔ Don't post this | Use the safe version instead. |

## Ordinary vs risky wording for health and beauty

Fine: questions and common, general knowledge ("Why do pimples come back?", "Coffee to get through study night?").
Not fine: diagnosing, promising an outcome, or naming a medical cause as certain ("This clears acne", "Your acne is caused by hormones").
