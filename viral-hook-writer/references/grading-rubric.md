# Hook Grading Rubric

Score each hook out of 10 using these 5 checks (2 points each).

| Check | 2 points | 1 point | 0 points |
|-------|----------|---------|----------|
| **Curiosity** – Does it make people want to know what's next? | Opens a clear question in the viewer's head | A little interesting | Nothing to wonder about |
| **Clarity** – Can a 12-year-old understand it instantly? | Simple, plain words | Slightly confusing | Jargon or hard to follow |
| **Speed** – Is it short and gets to the point? | Under 10 words | 10–15 words | Over 15 words or slow start |
| **Relevance** – Does the right viewer feel "this is about me"? | Names their exact problem or desire | Loosely related | Could be for anyone |
| **Contrast** – Does it surprise, challenge, or compare? | Clear surprise, myth, or "vs" | Mild twist | Totally expected |

## Score meaning

- **9–10:** Post it.
- **7–8:** Good. One small tweak could make it great.
- **5–6:** Okay. Needs a clearer problem or a stronger twist.
- **0–4:** Rewrite it using a template.
